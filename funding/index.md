# Funding opportunities

**Funding for UNM [students]({{ '/funding/grad-fellowships.html' | relative_url }}) and [faculty]({{ '/funding/seed-funding.html' | relative_url }}):** We fund community-engaged work through two annual funding competitions (info on [fellowships]({{ '/funding/grad-fellowships.html' | relative_url }}) and [classroom fund](https://canvas.unm.edu/courses/39553/pages) pages). In both competitions, priority is given to applicants committed to principles of community engagement that value local knowledge and support community-led work. 

**Funding for community partners:** We also directly fund work with community partners, primarily through external funding. If you have a project idea and would like to discuss the potential for working with us to find external funding through a university-community partnership, [contact us]({{ '/about/contact-us.html' | relative_url }})!
