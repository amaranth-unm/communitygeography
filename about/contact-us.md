# Keep in touch

The best way to keep in touch and learn about Center events and activities is to join our listserv. Click here to send an email to [listserv@list.unm.edu](mailto:listserv@list.unm.edu%20) from the address you wish to be subscribed. Make sure your message has a BLANK subject field, and include this message with no other text: subscribe communitygeog-L firstname lastname. \[You can also just [email us](mailto:communitygeography@unm.edu) and request that we add you, instead of doing it yourself!\]
