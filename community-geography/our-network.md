# Our Network

The Center for Community Geography's work aligns broadly with initiatives in Geography and Environmental Studies, Sustainability Studies, and other environment-focused departments and centers at UNM. The center also works in collaboration and partnership with organizations, agencies, and community members throughout New Mexico to address dynamic human-environment challenges and bring about positive change. If you would like to join our network, please contact us!

The Center for Community Geography is proud to have worked with the following partners:
