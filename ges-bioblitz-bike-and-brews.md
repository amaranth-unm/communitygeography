# GES BioBlitz Bike and Brews

<span id="photo-event"></span>[<img src="{{ '/ges_bioblitzbikeride.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Photo: GES BioBlitz Bike and Brews" />]({{ '/ges_bioblitzbikeride.jpg' | relative_url }})

Start:   
Apr 28, 2024 - 01:00pm

End:   
Apr 28, 2024 - 04:00pm

Location:   
Rio Grande Nature Center State Park,

You’re invited! 

The UNM GES Alumni Chapter is hosting our FIRST ANNUAL BioBlitz, Bike, and Brews event on Sunday, April 28<sup>th</sup> starting at the Rio Grande Nature Center State Park at 1pm and voyaging to the Boxing Bear Brewing Co. Corrales Taproom at 3 pm.  If you are not a biker, no problem, travel from the Nature Center to Boxing Bear using your preferred mode of transportation.

Bring yourself, your friends, and your family to catch up with the UNM Geography community and participate in the <a href="https://www.inaturalist.org/projects/unm-ges-city-nature-challenge-abq-2024" data-auth="NotApplicable" data-linkindex="0" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">2024 ABQ City Nature Challenge</a>.  Get outside!  Nerd out geography style! Socialize and network!  And, most importantly, enjoy free appetizers provided by the Alumni Chapter at the Boxing Bear Taproom between 3:00pm and 4:00pm as a thank you for coming and participating.

The Alumni Chapter is working hard to build events such as this to help build “The GES Alumni Chapter of the Future”, and we hope to see you at this event and more to come!

See the flier for more information. 

Spatial Thinkers Unite!
