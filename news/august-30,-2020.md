# Center for Community Geography adds staff member

*August 30, 2020*

Desiree Loggins joined the Center for Community Geography this month as a project assistant. Desiree is a new graduate student in the M.S. Geography program, where she is taking classes in the Environmental Studies concentration.

Alongside her academic work, Desiree will provide communications support for the new center, starting with the design and launch of a website and social media accounts. She will also provide general support for partnership outreach and project development. If you have ideas for partnerships or projects the center should investigate, we encourage you to get in touch with Desiree.

According to Center Director Maria Lane, Desiree’s academic interests are a great match for this position. “Desiree is interested in collective action, natural resource management, participatory planning, and environmental justice, so she’s a natural for this kind of position,” said Lane. “Plus she has a professional background working with nonprofits and community groups in New Mexico. We are delighted to have her on board.”

Prior to grad school, Desiree worked for six years at the National Audubon Society as a facilitator, organizer, and advocate for flowing rivers, working lands, and resilient communities in the arid West. Before that, she earned a B.A. in Environmental Studies from the University of California, Santa Cruz. Desiree now splits her time between Albuquerque and Zuni Pueblo, where she lives with her partner and two dogs. She is originally from California.
