# Upcoming Events

## 2026 Coffee Social - Dr. Michael DeVivo 

> **Start: **April 8, 2026 - 2:00pm **End:   **April 8, 2026 - 3:30pm **Presenter: **Michael DeVivo, PhD
>
> **Location: **UNM Campus Bandelier West 106

We are pleased to welcome Dr. Mike DeVivo to the R.H. Mallory Center for Community Geography for a Community Geography Social. This informal gathering offers an opportunity for students, faculty, and community members to connect with Dr. DeVivo, ask questions, and engage in relaxed conversation about geography, education, and his professional experiences. Food and light refreshments will be provided, and all are encouraged to stop by and take part in this welcoming and open setting. This event is intended to create space for meaningful dialogue and shared interests across the community. It also provides a chance to learn more about Dr. DeVivo’s work in an accessible and conversational environment. We hope attendees will take advantage of this opportunity to build connections and exchange ideas in a comfortable setting.

## **2026 Public Talk - Dr. Michael DeVivo**

> **Start: **April 10, 2026 - 3:00pm **End:   **April 10, 2026 - 4:30pm **Presenter: **Michael DeVivo, PhD **Location: **UNM Campus Bandelier East 105 

Mike DeVivo, Director of the Geography Program at Grand Rapids Community College and recipient of the AAG Presidential Achievement Award, will visit the University of New Mexico’s Department of Geography and Environmental Studies from April 8–10, 2025. As part of his visit, he will present a public lecture on geographic leadership and pedagogy on <img src="{{ '/events/dr-devivo.jpg' | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="338" height="338" alt="dr-devivo.jpg" />April 10, 2025. The lecture will draw on his experience developing a nationally recognized community college geography program. In addition to his public talk, DeVivo will meet with undergraduate and graduate students and engage faculty in conversations about best practices in geography education and community-engaged scholarship.

DeVivo is also the author of Leadership in American Academic Geography: The Twentieth Century and the creator of the “Conversations with a Geographer” interview series. A military veteran and accomplished photographer, he brings a broad perspective on leadership, service, and the discipline of geography.
