# Murphy Lecture 2023

Presenter:

Location:   
KIVA Lecture Hall

The Department of Geography and Environmental Studies will be hosting Dr. Lesley-Ann Dupigny-Giroux as the 2023 Murphy Lecturer. Dr. Dupigny-Giroux is a professor of Geography at the University of Vermont and also serves as the Vermont State Climatologist. 

The lecture will be held on Friday, November 17, from 3:00-4:00 p.m. in Mitchell Hall, Room 122. 

Refreshments will be served at 2:30 in the Bandalier Hall East lobby outside of room 105. 

Dr. Dupigny-Giroux's will give a presentation on the following: 

“Intentional Intersectionality: Exploring climate change through the lenses of vulnerability and inclusion”

In this presentation, Dr. Dupigny-Giroux will explore the ways in which researching and teaching about climate change concepts and response strategies can be explored with an emphasis on human and geographic vulnerabilities, so that all peoples and ways of knowing are honored and incorporated. Lessons will be drawn from her use of geospatial technologies in formal classroom settings as well as with lifelong learners to make climate change science inclusive and accessible.
