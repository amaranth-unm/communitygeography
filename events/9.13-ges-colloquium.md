# GES Colloquium

Start:

End:   
Sep 12, 2024 - 04:00pm

Presenter:   
Dr. Christopher Lippitt

Location:

We will kick off the departmental **Fall 2024 Colloquia Series** with a talk from our very own Dr. Chris Lippitt on ***Remote Sensing 2.0: Leveraging the IoT for persistent environmental monitoring*** (see flyer). The talk will be held in Bandelier East 105 from 3pm-4pm on Friday, September 13, followed by a social hour at Flock of Moons. We look forward to seeing you there! The talk is free and open to the public. Please share widely with your networks!
