# City Nature Challenge In Partnership with the Center for Community Geography

Start:   
Apr 25, 2025 - 09:30am

End:   
Apr 27, 2025 - 02:30pm

Presenter:   
Laurel Ladwig

Location:   
Smith Plaza South of Zimmerman Library, International District Library (7601 Central Ave. NE), Erna Fergusson Library (3700 San Mateo Dr. NE)

## **Friday, April 25th: **

 The Center for Community Geography will be tabling in Smith Plaza south of Zimmerman Library from 9:30-2:30, stop by for a CNC/ iNaturalist tutorial, snacks, and stickers!

We will host **guided walks at 11am and 2pm** leaving from Smith Plaza

## **Sunday, April 27th: **

Join us to map urban wildlife in the International District- 

The group will meet at the **International District Library at 11am** to make observations in the area, then will head to Erna Fergusson Library to cover that area.
