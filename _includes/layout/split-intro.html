{% comment %} Split Intro — a large statement on the left, the explanation on the right. For the opening argument of a landing page, where a single sentence carries the claim and a paragraph or two does the qualifying. The two-column split keeps the claim large without making the reader work through a wall of type. Parameters: - intro: an object from front matter (required). Keys: kicker: small caps label above the heading (optional) title: the claim, set large (required) text: a string, or an array of paragraphs. Markdown works.
